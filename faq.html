Quick answers about sample vehicle listings, search and simulated checkout.

Are these real vehicles for sale?

No. These are illustrative sample listings. No seller is contacted, vehicles are not verified or available for purchase, and nothing is shipped or transferred.

Does checkout place a real order or charge me?

No. There are no card fields and no payment processor connected. Use the demo code DEMO-OK to see the simulated confirmation.

Can this list every vehicle in the world?

Not as a static sample catalog. Worldwide availability constantly changes. A complete, current marketplace would need reliable inventory feeds, seller participation and listing verification. This demo has examples across six vehicle categories.

How do I search or filter vehicles?

Search by make, model, year, type, specification or location. Use the category buttons to narrow the listings to cars, motorcycles, yachts, jets, ships or commercial vehicles.

How do I change the shop name, prices, or vehicle photos?

Open store-config.js in VS Code. Edit the name and product entries. Put your own vehicle pictures in the images folder, then set an entry's image to its relative path, such as images/my-car.jpg. The names, prices, model matches and specifications here are demo examples, not verified offers.

What sound plays after demo checkout?

The site's order-confirmation sound plays after a successful demo order. Use Replay success sound on the confirmation screen to hear it again.

Does the contact form send messages?

This demo has no server. If you set a support email in store-config.js, the Contact page can open your device's email app with a drafted message.
